Troubadour Key Principles

The Troubadour tells a Story.

A Story has its Chapters.

Chapters have theirs own Sounds.

Prepare your story before your session. At the table, each chapter opens its own mixing table, and every sound waits on a card, ready to fire under your hand.

1

Story

Sotry Title: The Drowned Abbey. It holds its chapters and its own soundbox of quick effects.

2

Chapter

A Chapter is your scene: the flooded nave, the crypt, the abbot's fury.

3

3 sort of Sounds

Music: one track at a time, crossfading. Ambiances: rain, fire, crowds, layered on loop. Events: bells, roars, explosions, on cue or at random.
4

Sound card

One sound with its own controls. Play, pause, fade it out, loop it. Its anatomy is just below.
