Troubadour

La Troupe des Troubadours

Every table has its tales, and every troubadour a few tricks up the sleeve.

This is the guild hall. The Recueil keeps its lore: tales left behind by other troubadours, with their music, their ambiences and their thunderclaps, ready to join your party tonight. Turn your own story into a .troubadour file and lay it beside them.

Here also stand the expansions born from the original Troubadour. Ballade gives you command of time: every sound of a chapter waits on the golden wire and falls at its exact moment. Map gives you command of space: sounds dwell in the places of your world, and the party hears them as it walks. Wield both, and the whole soundscape of your world answers to you.
