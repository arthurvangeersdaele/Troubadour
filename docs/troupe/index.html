La Troupe des Troubadours

Every table has its tales, and some of them deserve to travel.

Take up the lute. Rhythm your story into a .troubadour file, with its music, its ambiences and its thunderclaps, and lay it in the Recueil for every game master to find.

Then wander through the tales the other troubadours have left behind, and bring one home to your table tonight.
