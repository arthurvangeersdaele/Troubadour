Recueil

Tales left behind by the troubadours of La Troupe. Open one, take it to your table.

    Add your tale

    1. Rhythm your story in the Story builder or in the Troubadour app.
    2. Lay the .troubadour file in the Recueil on GitHub. You only need a GitHub account: GitHub prepares the pull request for you.
    3. Once it is accepted, your tale appears here for every troubadour.
