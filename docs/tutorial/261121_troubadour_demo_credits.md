# Troubadour demonstration: sound credits

The demonstration video (`261121_troubadour_demo.mp4`) plays The Forest Adventure, the discovery story of Troubadour, its Map world and its Ballade. Every sound comes from open-source games, used under their original licences. To paste in a video description, the licences ask for attribution.

La vidéo de démonstration joue The Forest Adventure, l'histoire découverte de Troubadour, son monde Map et sa Ballade. Chaque son provient de jeux open source, utilisés sous leur licence d'origine.
