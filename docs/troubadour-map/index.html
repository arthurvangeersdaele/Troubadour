Troubadour Map

Some stories are told chapter by chapter. Others are walked.

Troubadour Map is for the troubadours who draw their world: lay the sounds on the map, the forge in the village, the wolves in the woods, the music of the castle.

At the table, move the golden point where the party goes. Closer is louder, walls muffle, and the music changes where the party stops.

1

Draw your worldMaps, walls, a hundred places to lay or your own to draw, and ways from one map to the next.

2

Lay the sounds on itMusics, ambiances and events on the whole map or on a place, with how far each place is heard.

3

Move the party at the tableDrag the golden point: the sounds follow. Share a world as a .troubadour-map file.
